# Code of conduct

Schemen Gate welcomes technical disagreement, careful falsification, and
direct critique. Participation must remain professional and safe for the
people doing the work.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss evidence, code, claims, and tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and access
  needs.
- Keep security reports private until maintainers have coordinated disclosure.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, or keys.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to fork under
  the repository license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, and
retaliation against good-faith reporters are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, email
[ryan@sekos.ai](mailto:ryan@sekos.ai). For vulnerabilities, follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the project and its community. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed
and avoided.

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers,
release channels, and project events or communications where someone is acting
as a Schemen Gate representative.
